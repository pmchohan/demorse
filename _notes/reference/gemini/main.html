Input Mode

Mode: Single Key (Spacebar)

  • Short press = Dit (·)
  • Long press = Dah (-)
  • Pause to complete character
  • Longer pause for space/newline

Mode: Two Keys

  • Press J or Left Arrow for Dit (·)
  • Press K or Right Arrow for Dah (-)
  • Pause to complete character/word

Decoding Path

Dit (·) Dah (-)
