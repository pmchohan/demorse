Good to know

  • Any key works. Tab, Esc, F-keys and Ctrl/Alt/Cmd combos are ignored so browser shortcuts still work.
  • On a phone or tablet, use the on-screen key.
  • Pause after a letter and it commits. Pause longer and you get a space, then a new line.
  • Punctuation decodes too, it just isn't drawn on the tree.
